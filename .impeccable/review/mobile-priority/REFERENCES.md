Public pages were opened in installed Brave at 1440×1000 and 390×844 on 7 September 2026. The vault notes guided selection; current pages were visually inspected for this revision.

| Source | Observed principle | Adaptation in KARCHX |

| [Marc Lou](https://marclou.com/) | Visible author identity beside concise product descriptions; the mobile page begins with the author. | Reviewed as context. Its small mobile avatar and product catalogue were not carried over: the owner explicitly wants a large portrait and a single unnamed agent. |

The portrait's larger dimensions and centered mobile copy are direct user requirements. No third-party illustrations, text, code, metrics, testimonials, fonts or logos were imported. Reference viewing did not involve registration, payments or external form submissions; it was not a full interaction audit of those sites.
