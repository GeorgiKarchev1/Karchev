# KARCHX Growth Systems — mini PRD

## Outcome

Make the new offer understandable on the existing website and prepare an evidence-based, immediately usable first-client sales experiment in Bulgarian.

## Confirmed requirements [high confidence]

- Bulgaria first; no existing proof or personal business access.
- Growth Systems positioning, concrete initial outcome and bounded implementation.
- Research and compare niches; provide source-linked candidate contacts, qualification questions, pricing hypotheses and sales materials.
- Rewrite Bulgarian and English homepage, navigation, contact flow, footer and social/search metadata. AI remains a supporting capability.
- Preserve existing visual identity, portrait, motion, routes, calendar and functioning contact API.
- No fabricated case studies, revenue promises or automatically sent outreach.
- Preserve unrelated Content OS changes and the user's deleted PRODUCT.md.

## Proposed choices [medium confidence; validate through sales]

- Premium detailing as the initial outbound niche; enquiries-to-paid-jobs as the first journey.
- €350 diagnostic / €1,500 sprint / partner from €1,000, scoped individually and not publicly locked in.
- Broad founder-led business positioning on the main website; niche-specific outreach in documents.

## Acceptance

- Website states audience, problem, audit/sprint/partner path, implementation, measurement and client participation in plain language.
- No AI-agent purchase framing or free-AI-audit promises in the rendered main conversion flow.
- Both languages have coherent metadata and working mobile navigation, FAQ, language switch and form states.
- Sales package includes niche comparison, sourced candidate list with uncertainty labels, scripts, demo and delivery/case-study process.
- Build/type/lint and browser checks report actual outcomes; no real form messages during tests.
